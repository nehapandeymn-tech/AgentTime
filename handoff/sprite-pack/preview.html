Animation library & character research

Eight transparent PNG sheets, 322 measured frames. Front, back, left, right and two three-quarter views; walking and running in both side directions. Each character has idle, peaceful work, thinking, retry, strain, encounter, recovery, completion and rest poses.

Ice King appears only for an encounter. When you choose peaceful work, the companions read, snack or dance. All-rest uses a dedicated group huddle.

Finn’s golden sword, Scarlet informed his practice and salute. Jake’s relaxed personality and stretchy powers informed the sandwich, knot and shield poses. BMO’s games, imagination and musical play informed the console and dance poses.

Ice King / Simon Petrikov is more than an antagonist. His optional ice encounter ends with a friendly gesture; a separate drumming animation reflects his musical side. These status metaphors are original animation choices, not canonical scenes.
